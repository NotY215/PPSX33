# PPSX33 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in PPSX33 to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss technical disagreements calmly and focus on evidence.
- Accept constructive feedback and respect differing experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant and provide useful context when reporting problems.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy. Report concerns privately using the contact or reporting options listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Please do not post sensitive details in public issues.
