# Governance

PS3 Recompiler is currently maintained as an individual-led open-source project under the NotY215 account.

## Decisions

The maintainer coordinates releases, reviews contributions, manages the roadmap, and makes final decisions when consensus cannot be reached. Technical discussion and well-supported alternatives are welcome in issues and pull requests.

## Contributions

Changes are reviewed for correctness, maintainability, test coverage, project scope, and licensing. Contributions may be revised or declined when they conflict with project goals or introduce unverified claims.

## Changes to governance

This document may be updated as the project and contributor community grow.
